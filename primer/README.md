# Can You Trust Your Judge's Score? A Primer on TPR and TNR for LLM Judges

Published at https://bfalkowski.github.io/writing/judge-score.html. `tpr-tnr.html` is the source for that page.

A short explainer that later papers link back to for definitions.

- `primer.md`: the text, in Markdown.
- `tpr-tnr.html`: the page for the site, with interactive widgets in Sections 5 and 10. The widgets' formulas match
  the worked examples in the text.

Section anchors for links from other papers: `#question`, `#label`, `#tpr-tnr`, `#improve`, `#correction`,
`#range`, `#report`, `#models`, `#rules`, `#summary`, `#glossary`.

The primer follows one invented example (is this customer service reply polite?) from labeling to reporting.
`numbers.py` recomputes every number in it, including the Section 6 intervals. `range.py` is the short
version of the 95% range calculation shown in Appendix A.
